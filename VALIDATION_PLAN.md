## Decision rule for the prototype

1. Compare rank order and reasons; do not optimize weights on only a few reviewers.
2. Investigate cases where the app marks a facility low while practitioners mark it urgent, especially remote sites and missing-data cases.
3. Record every change to weights, labels and decision support text, then rerun the scenarios.
4. Treat 3 usability sessions as formative feedback only. They do not establish usability or safety.
5. Before any real pilot: obtain local health/energy authorization, data governance and privacy review, clinically safe operating procedures, authenticated access and a representative prospective dataset.
